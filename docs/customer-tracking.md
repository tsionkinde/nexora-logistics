# Nexora Logistics - Customer Tracking

## 1. Purpose

The customer tracking feature allows customers to monitor the progress of their shipments without needing access to internal logistics management functions.

Customers should be able to view shipment information using a unique tracking number.

## 2. Tracking Access Approach

The initial MVP will use a public tracking page with a unique, secure tracking number.

Customers will not need to log in just to check the basic status of a shipment.

Example:

```text
https://your-domain.com/track/TRACKING_NUMBER
```
